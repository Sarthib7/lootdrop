How it works
01

Drop a reward

Reply to a member, or name them: /reward 10 great work.

02

Policy decides

Small amounts auto-approve; larger get an Approve button. Budgets, cooldowns, audit — built in.

03

They claim it

The recipient picks privately and gets a real code. A redacted line posts back to the group.
