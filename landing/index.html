Three steps
1

Drop a reward

Reply to a member, or just name them: /reward 10 great bug report.

2

Policy decides

BountyGuard auto-approves small amounts; bigger ones get an Approve button. Budgets, cooldowns and audit are built in.

3

They claim it

The recipient picks a reward privately and gets a real code — a redacted line posts back to the group.
