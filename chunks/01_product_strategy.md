# 01 — Product Strategy

LootDrop should not be pitched as a privacy-payment app. Privacy is a secondary benefit framed as convenience:

```txt
no bank details needed
no payout headache
recipient chooses
instant digital delivery
```

The strongest wedge is Discord/gaming communities.

Why this creates value for Bitrefill:

1. One community admin introduces many recipients to Bitrefill.
2. Recipients browse Bitrefill products after earning a reward.
3. Agents get a concrete commerce workflow.
4. BountyGuard makes agent spending safer.
5. The flow can grow into partner/affiliate volume.

Differentiation:

```txt
Bitrefill MCP/API: agent can buy products.
LootDrop: agent/community can safely reward people with claims, approvals, recipient choice, and logs.
```

Competitive wedge:

```txt
Tremendous/Tango/Giftbit = enterprise reward platforms.
LootDrop = chat-native, community-first, agent-native reward claims.
```
